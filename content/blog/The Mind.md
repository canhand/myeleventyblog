The mind tricks the user - me in this case.

The ability to control the mind or hold-the-reins (so to speak) is of utmost importance.

---

I recently read something on the lines of -
> If one can't take hold of their mind, they can't get anything meaningful done.

---
Couldn't be more true.

My personal struggle has been with a mind that does it's own thing. But how could that be? This very realisation is coming from the same mind too right!

Normally, I would've spent hours if not days browsing the internet, reading snippets or books, trying to understand why it's so.

Even now, a large part of me wants to speed up that process with a little bit of AI - a tool for massive intellectual hedonism, right on my fingertips.

--- 

Curbing that urge for now though.

---

*Eureka!*

Maybe that's it.

That thing I just did. Curbed an urge, chose what to focus on - or at least arrived at this point through some form of learning/training etc.
