## Set and setting

Old habits die hard.

I always end up forming a layer of things that are very routine in nature - probably everyone does. The reason I mention it is not to sound binary. There are some who follow a routine to the T. Part of me is that person.

The other part that isn't makes me wonder if it's counter-productive.

At the end, it's just about habits.

When a significant amount of time is spent perfecting lack of output, with the use of a routine, it becomes a habit much like anything else.

## Learing

Today I've learnt, for sure, that the way forward is to reconcile the two parts and use it to my advantage.

**Changing oneself is next to impossible. Changing how one thinks about themselves and spends time on is definitely possible.**
